Lender Cash Flow Schedule

lender's perspective, ties to Variable Profit
Month 0: funding drawn offsets the disbursement paid to the customer (net zero); PF Income, Cross Sell Income, CAC, Processing Costs and CGTMSE Costs are recognized here, at origination. Months 1+: Customer Collection = Payment at IRR; Finance Cost Serviced = debt service at Finance Cost Rate. Salary Costs are spread evenly across every month. Credit Costs (one-time write-off) and NPA Interest Reversal (month-by-month) both start at the presumed-NPA point — Tenure/2 — used above. Cumulative Cash Flow at the final month reconciles exactly to Variable Profit.
